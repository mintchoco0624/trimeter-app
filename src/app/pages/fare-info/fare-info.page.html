Fare complaint assistance

  1. Compare the fare on your TriMeter receipt with what the driver asked. Stay calm and show the receipt.
  2. Note the tricycle’s body number or plate number, its TODA, and the date, time and route of the trip.
  3. Save or share the receipt. It shows the distance, the fare breakdown and the fare data version used.
  4. Report the case to the Cabanatuan City Government (City Hall), which regulates tricycle franchises.
